A strong writing engagement includes

  • A clear reader and outcome
  • A chapter-by-chapter logic
  • Agreed voice and tone examples
  • Regular review milestones
  • Documented decisions and revisions

Writing options

Choose the collaboration model that fits your time and voice.

  1. 1

    Ghostwriting

    You provide subject matter, interviews, source material, and feedback. We develop the manuscript in an agreed voice.

  1. 2

    Collaborative writing

Typical workflow

From concept to complete manuscript

  1. 1

    Discovery and positioning

    Define the audience, problem, promise, format, voice, and business or creative purpose.

  2. 2

    Outline and sample

    Create a detailed outline and sample section so direction is approved before full drafting.

8
  1. 3

    Research and interviews

    Gather source material, examples, stories, evidence, and original insight in an organized way.

  2. 4

    Milestone drafting

    Draft in planned sections, review feedback, and prevent late-stage structural surprises.

  3. 5

    Revision and editorial handoff

    Complete author revisions, confirm the manuscript, and move into the editing workflow.
