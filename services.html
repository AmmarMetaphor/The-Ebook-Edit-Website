03

For complete drafts with structural doubts

Developmental editing

Solves problems of argument, order and depth before any sentence polishing. You provide the full draft; we examine how the book works as a whole.

  • Chapter-level comments and analysis
  • Structural recommendations
  • Content additions, cuts and reordering
  • A prioritised revision roadmap

You receive: a marked manuscript and editorial letter. You revise next, with a clear plan.

04

For settled structure, unpolished prose

Line editing

Refines how the writing reads: rhythm, clarity, tone, emphasis and flow, sentence by sentence — while protecting the personality of your voice.

  • Sentence-level rewriting and tightening
  • Tracked changes with editorial queries
  • Tone and transition refinement
  • Voice preserved and strengthened

You receive: a refined manuscript that still sounds unmistakably like you.

05

For stable manuscripts needing correctness

Copy editing

Brings professional accuracy and consistency once the content is settled. You provide the revised draft; we correct and standardise it line by line.

  • Grammar, punctuation and usage
  • Consistency of terms, names and numbers
  • Cross-references and internal logic
  • A project style sheet

You receive: a corrected manuscript and the style sheet that keeps future updates consistent.

8
06

For formatted books before release

Proofreading

The final quality check after editing and formatting are complete — catching remaining errors rather than reworking the writing.

  • Typos, punctuation and spacing
  • Formatting and heading consistency
  • Navigation, links and page breaks
  • Final file review

You receive: a clean, publication-ready text. Proofreading is a last check, not a substitute for editing.

Ebook formatting and conversion

Turns the finished text into clean, technically sound files that read comfortably on every major device and store.

  • EPUB conversion and cleanup
  • Kindle-compatible preparation

  • Validation and device checks

You receive: upload-ready files plus the organised source manuscript.

13

How engagements work

Every service answers the same three questions.

  • P

    What you provide

    Source material, one consolidated set of feedback per milestone, and approvals at agreed decision points.

  • R

    What you receive

    Defined deliverables — manuscripts, letters, style sheets, files — organised and explained, never a vague handover.

  • N

    What happens next

    A recommendation for the following stage, with no obligation to continue. Scope is agreed in writing before work begins.
