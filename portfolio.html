Editorial possibilities across different kinds of ebooks.

These representative project types show how writing, editing, formatting and publishing support can be shaped around different books. They illustrate typical approaches rather than completed client engagements.

Representative project types

Three ways the process can be applied.

Nonfiction

Representative project type

Expertise-led nonfiction

Turn specialist knowledge, interviews or source material into a structured nonfiction ebook designed around a clear reader outcome.

Typical focus

  • Positioning and reader promise
  • Interview-led or collaborative writing
  • Chapter structure and editorial refinement

Typical deliverables

A coherent manuscript, editorial files and publication-ready handoff materials.

Practical guide

Representative project type

Reader-friendly practical guide

Shape educational or instructional material into an accessible guide that helps readers move from information to action.

Typical focus

  • Content organisation and navigation
  • Tone, clarity and accessibility
  • Callouts, resources and supporting sections

Typical deliverables

A polished guide prepared for digital formatting and publishing.

Fiction

Representative project type

Narrative fiction

Strengthen story structure and sentence-level craft while preserving the author’s narrative voice.

Typical focus

  • Character motivation and continuity
  • Scene pacing and dialogue
  • Line editing, copy editing and proof review

Typical deliverables

A refined manuscript with clear editorial notes and final quality checks.
