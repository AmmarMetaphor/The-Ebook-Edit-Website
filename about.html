Our point of view

Good editorial work is both creative and disciplined.

It asks the big questions—why this book, for whom, and in what order—then pays equal attention to the small ones: wording, punctuation, headings, links, metadata, and file quality.

The goal is not to make every manuscript sound the same. The goal is to help each one become more intentional, readable, and ready for its audience.

Editorial values

How the work is approached

  • C

    Clarity

    Readers should understand the promise, structure, and next step without unnecessary friction.

  • V

    Voice

    Editing should strengthen expression while preserving the author’s personality and intent.

  • R

    Rigor

    Claims, consistency, source handling, details, and final files deserve careful attention.

  • T

    Transparency

    Scope, milestones, questions, trade-offs, and responsibilities should be understandable.

What we do not promise

Credibility matters.

We do not invent testimonials, guarantee sales, promise bestseller status, or publish unverified claims. Marketing and editorial decisions should remain accurate, ethical, and supportable.

How we work

One managed process, however much of it you need.

Projects run through a six-stage process with written scope, milestones and approval points. You can enter at any stage — strategy, writing, editing, formatting or publishing preparation — and leave with organised, usable files.

Where we work

The Ebook Edit is operated by Inovantage Ltd and works remotely with authors and organisations across the UK and US. Email support@theebookedit.com.

How quality is managed

Quality is a system, not a mood.

  • A project style sheet records every editorial decision
  • Separate editing passes for structure, sentences and correctness
  • Checklists before formatting, and again before publication
  • Documented queries, so nothing is changed silently
  • Organised source files delivered for future editions
