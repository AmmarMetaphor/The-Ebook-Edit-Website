Privacy

Privacy policy

How The Ebook Edit collects, uses and protects the information you share with us. Last updated August 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Inovantage Ltd, trading as The Ebook Edit. For privacy questions or requests about your personal information, contact info@theebookedit.com.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to send us: the details submitted through the project enquiry form (such as your name, email address, project stage and project description), the contents of emails you send us, and messages you choose to send through WhatsApp. This website does not require an account and does not collect payment information.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver services you request, keep normal business records, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Legal bases

Where UK data protection law applies, we rely on your consent when you send us an enquiry, the performance of a contract when we deliver services to you, and our legitimate interests in responding to messages and keeping accurate business records.

Hosting and form processing

This website is hosted by Netlify, which also processes and stores contact form submissions on our behalf. Messages sent through WhatsApp are processed by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not run any analytics script. If analytics or similar tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated first and any legally required consent mechanism will be put in place.

Retention

We keep enquiry and project correspondence for as long as it is needed to respond to you, deliver the work, and maintain reasonable business records — and no longer than necessary. You may ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact address above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. UK residents may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). To exercise any right, email info@theebookedit.com.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information outside the country you contact us from — for example, Netlify processes data in the United States. Providers are required to protect personal information under their applicable safeguards and terms.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised.
