Our process

A structured path that keeps creative work moving.

Every project is different, but each one benefits from clear stages, decision points, responsibilities, and version control.

2

Step by step

Six stages that keep the project on track.

  • 1Project discovery
  • 2Review and scope
  • 3Strategy and planning
  • 4Creation and review
  • 5Editorial completion
  • 6Formatting and publication
3

1. Project discovery

We discuss your goal, audience, source material, manuscript stage, desired services, timeline, and publishing route.

The Ebook EditReviews your goals and material.
YouShare objectives, source material and constraints.

Output: project recommendation and information request.

4

2. Review and scope

We review relevant files, identify the real level of work, define deliverables, establish milestones, and confirm what is outside scope.

The Ebook EditDiagnoses the work and defines scope.
YouConfirm priorities and approve the proposal.

Output: written proposal, timeline, and responsibilities.

5

3. Strategy and planning

For writing or developmental projects, we define positioning, outline, chapter goals, voice, source plan, and review cadence.

The Ebook EditDevelops direction, outline and voice.
YouApprove audience, promise and outline.

Output: approved project brief and production plan.

6

4. Creation and review

Writing or editing proceeds in agreed milestones. Feedback is consolidated so decisions remain clear and revisions stay efficient.

The Ebook EditWrites or edits the manuscript in milestones.
YouProvide one consolidated set of feedback per milestone.

Output: milestone drafts and review notes.

7

5. Editorial completion

The manuscript moves through the appropriate editing sequence, author review, final text confirmation, and proofreading.

The Ebook EditCompletes the editorial sequence.
YouReview and approve the final manuscript.

Output: approved final manuscript and style record.

8

6. Formatting and publication

Final files, navigation, and metadata are prepared for platforms such as Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP), Apple Books, Kobo Writing Life, Google Play Books, Barnes & Noble Press, and Draft2Digital, then quality checked against a publishing checklist.

The Ebook EditPrepares files, metadata and the readiness checklist.
YouControl the publishing account and approve the upload.

Output: organised publishing package.

What helps a project succeed

A few simple working agreements make a major difference.

Clear communication

Receive clear updates, decisions and next steps throughout the project.

Collaborative checkpoints

Feedback and approvals are built into each major stage before the work moves forward.

Organised delivery

Final manuscript, formatting files and publishing materials are supplied in a clear, usable package.
