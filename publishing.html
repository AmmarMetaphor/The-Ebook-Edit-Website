Formatting

Readable interiors across common ebook formats.

Formatting is more than making pages look attractive. It should create hierarchy, navigation, consistency, and a comfortable reading experience across devices.

  • EPUB conversion and cleanup
  • PDF interior layout
  • Clickable table of contents
  • Front matter and back matter
  • Heading, list, image, and link styling
  • File validation and visual checks

Typical deliverables

  • ✓Final EPUB file
  • ✓Final PDF file where required
  • ✓Organized source manuscript
  • ✓Upload-ready cover assets supplied by you or your designer
  • ✓Final quality checklist

Publishing support

Reduce uncertainty around the final steps.

M

Metadata

Develop a clear title/subtitle presentation, book description, author bio, keywords, categories, and version notes.

U

Upload preparation

Organize files and work through a platform-specific checklist before the book is submitted.

Q

Quality assurance

Review links, navigation, page breaks, images, front matter, back matter, and preview output.

L

Launch materials

Prepare the core copy needed for a landing page, email announcement, social posts, and partner outreach.

V

Version control

Keep editable source files, final files, edition notes, and update records organized for future revisions.

G

Guided handoff

Receive a written summary of files, settings, ownership, and recommended post-launch checks.

Before publication

A final readiness checklist

Editorial sign-off

All major revisions are complete and the final text has been proofread.

Rights and permissions

Quotes, images, data, and third-party material are cleared for use.

Metadata approved

Title, description, author information, keywords, and categories are consistent.

Files tested

Navigation, links, layout, images, and readability are checked in relevant previews.

Launch ownership clear

Accounts, passwords, payment details, files, and responsibilities remain under the author or publisher’s control.
