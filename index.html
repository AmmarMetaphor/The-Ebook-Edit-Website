A calm, clear workflow

How your ebook moves forward

A six-stage process keeps the work focused while leaving room for insight, revision and creative decisions.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Define the reader, purpose, scope, voice, timeline, and success criteria.

  2. 2

    Plan

    Build the outline, chapter logic, research plan, and production schedule.

  3. 3

    Create

    Write, edit, review, and revise through structured milestones.

12
  1. 4

    Polish

    Complete copyediting, proofreading, formatting, and quality checks.

  2. 5

    Publish

    Prepare files, metadata, and upload steps for Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP), Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play Books, and other platforms.

  3. 6

    Keep evolving

    Retain organised source files so updates and new editions stay manageable.
